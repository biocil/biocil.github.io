Settings
  1. Face a window or lamp so your face is evenly lit; avoid flicker from dim LED bulbs.
  2. Fill about half the frame with your face and keep talking to a minimum.
  3. Wait 5–10 s for the reading to settle. Small hand shake is corrected; big moves pause the estimate.
