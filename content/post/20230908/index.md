Tuba Oğuz’s poster, *Holotomographic Imaging of Myelinated Axons in the CNS* (with Bilal Ersen Kerman and Muhammed Fatih Toy), won second place in the best poster competition at FOTONİK 2023, the National Optics, Electro-Optics and Photonics Workshop, held at Koç University in Istanbul on 8 September 2023. The same line of work appears in our [Scientific Reports paper on label-free holotomography of brain myelin](/publication/2026_scirep_oguz/).
