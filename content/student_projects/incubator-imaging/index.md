A senior-year graduation project in Biomedical Engineering at Istanbul Medipol University (2026–2027), supervised by Fatih Toy. The team is designing and building an incubator that keeps mammalian cells under culture conditions while imaging them, so that living cells can be followed over time without being taken out of their environment.
