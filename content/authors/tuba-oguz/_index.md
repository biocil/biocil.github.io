Tuba Oğuz was a co-supervised student working on label-free holotomographic imaging of myelin, together with Bilal E. Kerman. Her poster on holotomographic imaging of myelinated axons won a prize at FOTONİK 2023, and the work appears in the [2026 Scientific Reports paper](/publication/2026_scirep_oguz/).
