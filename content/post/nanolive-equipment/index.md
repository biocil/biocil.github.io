Nanolive SA contributed a 3D Cell Explorer holotomographic microscope and two Basler CMOS cameras to the lab. The Cell Explorer images the 3D refractive index of living cells without labels and complements our own [holotomography](/research/holotomography/) set-ups. Thank you, Nanolive!

Fatih Toy co-founded Nanolive SA in 2013 and remains an advisor to the company, without a salary.
