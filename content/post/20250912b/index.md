The lab took two posters to FOTONİK 2025, the National Optics, Electro-Optics and Photonics Workshop, held at Koç University in Istanbul on 12 September 2025:

- **Enhancing Jones Matrix Reconstruction in Polarization DHM via Phase Bias Equalization**, Mohammad M. T. R. Hussein and M. Fatih Toy, which [won third place in the best poster competition](/post/20250912/).
- **Spiral Phase Plate Based Optical Edge Enhancement**, Maha Sahloul and M. Fatih Toy.
