From R to SpO₂ (empirical calibration)

How it works

  1. Blood volume pulses with each heartbeat → absorbed light pulses (AC on top of DC).
  2. Deoxy-Hb absorbs red (660 nm) ~10× more than oxy-Hb; at 940 nm oxy-Hb absorbs a bit more.
  3. R = (AC/DC)red ÷ (AC/DC)IR cancels skin, bone and LED brightness.
  4. SpO₂ ≈ 110 − 25·R (typical calibration curve, measured on volunteers).
