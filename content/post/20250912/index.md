Mohammad M. T. R. Hussein’s poster, *Enhancing Jones Matrix Reconstruction in Polarization DHM via Phase Bias Equalization* (with M. Fatih Toy), won third place in the best poster competition at FOTONİK 2025, the National Optics, Electro-Optics and Photonics Workshop, held at Koç University in Istanbul on 12 September 2025. Congratulations, Mohammad!
