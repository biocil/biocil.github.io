Nanolive SA, the Swiss company behind the 3D Cell Explorer, contributed one of its holotomographic microscopes and two Basler CMOS cameras to the lab. The 3D Cell Explorer measures the 3D refractive index of living cells without labels, and we use it alongside our own [holotomography](/research/holotomography/) set-ups.

**Disclosure.** Fatih Toy co-founded Nanolive SA in 2013 and remains an advisor to the company, without a salary.
