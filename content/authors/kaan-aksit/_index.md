Kaan Akşit is an associate professor of computational light in the Computer Science Department of University College London, where he leads the [Computational Light Laboratory](https://complightlab.com). His research sits where light meets computation, including AI-assisted methods for imaging, graphics, fabrication and displays, and he is best known for his work on displays for 3D, virtual and augmented reality.

He completed his PhD in electrical engineering at Koç University in 2014, after an MSc in electrical power engineering at RWTH Aachen University and a BSc in electrical engineering at Istanbul Technical University. He interned at Philips Research and Disney Research, and from 2014 to 2020 was a senior research scientist at NVIDIA. He is an Optica Fellow, a senior member of IEEE and SPIE, and an associate editor of ACM Transactions on Graphics.

With the Phi-X lab he co-authored the Optics Continuum paper on [all-optical selective target highlighting for augmented-reality microscopy](/publication/2026_optcon_sahloul/), which was selected for the journal’s cover.

More on [his personal website](https://kaanaksit.com) and the [Computational Light Laboratory website](https://complightlab.com).
