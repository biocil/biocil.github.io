On 13 August 2026, Mohammad M. T. R. Hussein defended his MSc thesis in Biomedical Engineering, *Polarization-sensitive digital holographic microscopy with structured illumination*, supervised by Fatih Toy.

His work produced a dual-shot polarization holographic microscope that recovers the full-field Jones matrix of a sample from two exposures, published in [Biomedical Optics Express](/publication/2026_bopex_hussein/). Congratulations, Mohammad!
