Our first entry in the new [Fun](/fun/) corner: [sesameseeds](/fun/sesameseeds/), a LaTeX package that makes your manuscript look as if it was read over a simit. Seeds and crumbs land on every page, reproducibly, and you can sprinkle more by hand or leave the ring where the simit was put down. Afiyet olsun.
