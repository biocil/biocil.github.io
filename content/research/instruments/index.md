Much of our work ends up as a physical instrument. Some examples:

- **Culture monitoring.** A low-cost top-stage incubator with integrated lens-free holographic imaging lets cells be watched continuously without moving them out of their environment.
- **Spectrometers.** Speckle-enhanced prism spectrometers combine a simple prism with a scattering element to reach high resolution and dynamic range in a compact package (US Patent 11,231,322).
- **Fabrication with holographic feedback.** Maskless lithography that checks the part being made with holography during fabrication, for producing optical elements.
- **Microscope autofocus** (WO 2024/208601), and real-time holographic tracking and control of microrobots.

Undergraduate teams build working devices too; see the [student projects](/research/#sp-title).
