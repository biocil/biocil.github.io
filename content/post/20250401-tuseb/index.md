Our undergraduate researchers [Emirhan Pehlevan](/author/emirhan-pehlevan/) and [Yunus Emre Özel](/author/yunus-emre-ozel/) won support from the Health Institutes of Türkiye (TÜSEB) for their project *Development of a Low-Cost and Easily Accessible Pathology Tissue Section Scanner* (Düşük Maliyetli ve Kolay Erişilebilir Bir Patoloji Doku Kesiti Tarayıcısının Geliştirilmesi), under the TÜSEB 2025-A1-01 R&D programme for undergraduate students. Congratulations, Emirhan and Yunus Emre!
